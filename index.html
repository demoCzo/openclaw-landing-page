Frequently asked questions

What is Claude and how does it work?

Claude is an AI assistant from Anthropic that helps you reason, write, code, and research. You chat in plain language; Claude responds with grounded, steerable answers and can use tools when enabled.

What should I use Claude for?

Drafting and editing, brainstorming, learning complex topics, turning notes into plans, building and debugging code, and analyzing documents or images — always with human review for high-stakes decisions.

How much does it cost to use?

There is a free tier for getting started. Paid plans add higher usage limits, Cowork, Claude Code, and other productivity features. Enterprise options include admin and security controls.
